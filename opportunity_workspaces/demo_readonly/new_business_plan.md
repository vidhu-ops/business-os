# IIDATECH Agentic Business Plan — CRM automation for SMBs (India)

**Company type:** New venture · **Geography:** India · **Industry:** SaaS / B2B Software

## 1. Executive summary

**Pipely CRM** is a WhatsApp-first CRM automation platform for Indian SMBs in clinics, coaching, and real estate. Pricing from ₹999/seat/month with Hindi/English UI and vertical playbooks.

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Paying accounts | 120 | 1,400 |
| ARPU (monthly) | ₹1,499 | ₹1,899 |
| ARR | ₹21.6 L | ₹3.2 Cr |

**Ask:** ₹1.2 Cr seed for 18-month runway and GTM in Mumbai + Bangalore.

## 2. Problem

Indian SMBs lose 30–40% of inbound leads because follow-up lives in personal WhatsApp and Excel. Enterprise CRM is overbuilt for 5–25 seat teams.

## 3. Solution

- Unified WhatsApp + email inbox
- Pipeline, tasks, SLA reminders
- Automation builder for drip sequences
- Vertical kits: clinic recall, coaching trials, real-estate site visits

## 4. Go-to-market

Design partners (M1–3) → vertical launch (M4–9) → channel scale (M10–18). CAC target ₹8–12k, payback under 8 months.

## 5. 90-day roadmap

**Days 1–30:** Clinic MVP + 5 design partners  
**Days 31–60:** Billing, Hindi UI, 3 case studies  
**Days 61–90:** CA partner program, ₹2L MRR target
